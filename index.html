    Signal methodology and exit rules

    Entry is within ±0.1 ATR of the last completed close. The calculator assumes the highest price in that zone. Stop is the lower of 1.5 ATR below that close and the five-candle low minus 0.1 ATR. ATR is the simple mean of 14 true ranges. TP1 and TP2 are mechanical 1R/2R levels after your cost assumptions; they are not additional model forecasts.

    The model endpoint must support at least 2:1 net reward/risk and exceed historical MAE. All three sampled endpoints must cover costs. Ten 24h holdouts, MAE at least 10% below the persistence baseline, and directional accuracy ≥55% are illustrative screening thresholds, not statistical proof or a probability of profit.

    A fresh same-exchange quote must be inside the entry zone. No entry after expiry, and no chasing a missed setup. Hypothetical exits: stop all remaining units, take half at TP1, the rest at TP2, or exit any remainder at the forecast horizon. If both targets fill, the 50/50 split yields 1.5R under the cost assumptions, not 2R for the entire position. No averaging down, leverage, or automatic stop adjustment.

    The backtests above measure forecast accuracy. This entry/stop/target strategy has not been backtested for fills, net returns or drawdowns. No holding recommendation is made for existing positions. Read about stop-order execution risk ↗
