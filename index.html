TRANSPARENCY, BY DESIGN

Research with the uncertainty left in.

Forecasts are stochastic model outputs, not real market prices, investment advice or trading signals. Sample paths are not calibrated confidence intervals. A short retrospective backtest does not establish future performance, and overlap with model pretraining data is unknown. Predicted candle wicks are expanded to enclose raw OHLC values; predicted closes are unchanged.
